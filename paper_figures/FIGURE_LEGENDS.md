# Figure legends

Use these legends together with the methods. Figure numbers are package numbers and should be renumbered to match the manuscript. All panels are original, deterministic model visualizations.

**Figure 1 | Anatomical context of calculated temporal-interference modulation.** **a–c**, Orthographic lateral, coronal and dorsal cutaways through the left VPL-centred planes (ML=-2.00, AP=-1.40 and DV=-4.30 mm, respectively) in the generic atlas coordinate frame. Grey surfaces show the Allen CCF brain; gold surfaces show the anatomical left VPL; crosses mark the requested target at AP=-1.94, ML=-1.75 and DV=-3.10 mm. The colour layer is a calculated plane section, not a field isosurface. Values are divided by the same baseline whole-brain maximum and displayed on a logarithmic scale. I1=I2=0.50 mA; f1=2000 Hz and f2=2010 Hz. Scale bars, 2 mm in the projected image plane. These are homogeneous-conductor analytic previews, not registered FEM or neuronal activation maps.

**Figure 2 | Requested-target and VPL-centred orthogonal sections.** **a–c**, Sagittal, coronal and axial planes through the requested target. **d–f**, Corresponding planes through the rounded atlas VPL centroid coordinates. Dashed contours identify the left VPL where it intersects a plane; crosses in a–c identify the requested point. In c, the requested axial plane does not intersect the VPL mask. The distinction is retained rather than removed by target snapping. Axes are generic bregma coordinates in millimetres. All panels share the baseline normalization and logarithmic colour scale from Fig. 1. Brain geometry restricts visualization and voxel sampling, not the conductor's boundary conditions.

**Figure 3 | Ideal electrode montage and channel contributions.** **a**, Requested electrode centres on the ideal DV=+0.50 mm plane. Blue markers represent E1/E2 (AP pair) and orange markers E3/E4 (ML pair). Dashed connections indicate channel pairing, not current streamlines or inserted electrode trajectories. The plane is a geometric reference, not a reconstructed skull. **b,c**, Magnitudes of the two independently calculated carrier fields on the AP=-1.40 mm coronal plane. **d**, Maximum-direction TI modulation depth on the same plane. All three scalar quantities use the same denominator, the baseline whole-brain maximum mTI; the shared logarithmic colour scale extends to 3. I1=I2=0.50 mA. A large carrier field or modulation value does not on its own establish neuronal recruitment.

**Figure 4 | Effect of current redistribution in the analytic model.** **a–c**, Axial TI maps at DV=-4.30 mm for I1:I2 ratios of 0.25:0.75, 0.50:0.50 and 0.75:0.25 mA. Total current is held at 1.00 mA, and the baseline denominator and colour limits are unchanged. **d**, Left VPL mean, requested-point value and whole-brain 95th percentile over a prespecified 19-point current sweep. Curves are deterministic model summaries with no biological replicates, confidence intervals or significance tests. Current redistribution changes the spatial envelope, but this comparison does not establish VPL-selective stimulation or an optimal montage.

**Figure 5 | Frequency difference controls temporal modulation in the resistive model.** **a–c**, Projected carrier signals (grey) and exact positive/negative envelope curves (blue) at the requested target for frequency differences of 10, 40 and 100 Hz. The first carrier is fixed at 2000 Hz. The projection is the bisector of the two equal-current target fields, and traces are normalized by the sum of their positive projected amplitudes. A nonzero frequency difference changes the beat rate while leaving the spatial envelope calculation unchanged in this frequency-independent resistive approximation. Equal carrier frequencies yield no difference-frequency modulation. No neuronal membrane or neural response is simulated.

**Figure 6 | Target-to-atlas correspondence and descriptive field distributions.** **a**, Enlarged view of the requested target, atlas left VPL and nearest VPL voxel centre. The 0.636 mm separation is a three-dimensional point-to-voxel-centre distance under the generic transform. It is not a measured registration residual or the exact distance to a continuous anatomical boundary. **b**, Empirical cumulative distributions of normalized mTI over 505,359 labelled brain voxels and 441 left VPL voxels at 100 µm spacing. The voxel populations describe the model's spatial distribution and are not independent animals. The atlas VPL does not contain the whole-brain maximum in this baseline calculation.
